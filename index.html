Returning movement. Returning life.

A myoelectric arm for below-elbow amputees.

Thailand has roughly 200,000 people living with upper-limb disability, and around 70% of them are out of work.1 A working myoelectric arm here costs between 300,000 and 2,740,000 baht. We build one for 100,000.

1 Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Thailand — ~2.2 million registered people with disabilities; ~200,000 with an upper-limb disability.

The design

A full hand — four fingers, an opposed thumb, every part our own.

This is not an off-the-shelf gripper with a cosmetic cover. Every element — the finger linkages, the opposed thumb, the palm chassis that houses the control board, and the socket — is designed in-house and printed as one system.

Designing the whole assembly ourselves is what lets us hold the arm to 0.65 kg, service it without an imported spare, and move the price where a competitor buying their parts cannot.

What the six told us

Our best score validates the control. Our worst score is why we keep working.

Every fitted user rated the arm on a five-point scale. We are publishing all of it, including the number we like least.

Fitted-user assessment · n = 6 · 1–5 scale, mean
MeasureMean
EMG control accuracy 4.33
Grip satisfaction 4.17
Arm strength 3.50
Socket comfort 3.50
Arm weight 3.00

Control scored highest. That is the result that matters most to us — it says a single EMG channel, done carefully, is enough to give someone a grip they can rely on.

Weight scored lowest. Every one of the six gave it a 3. We are already the lightest arm in our category and our own users still call it heavy. That is not a number we argue with — it is the one that sets our research agenda.

Six users is a product study, not a clinical trial. There is no controlled outcome data yet — no function score, no wear-time, no protocol. Getting that is our next piece of work, and it is why we are looking for a clinical research partner.

Scope

What ships, and what is still in the lab.

Prosthetics is a field with a long history of demonstrations that never reached a patient. We would rather be precise about the line.

Shipping today

  • Single-channel surface EMG
  • One grip, actuated reliably
  • 3D-scanned individual socket
  • In-house control board
  • SLS PA12 shell, 0.65 kg

In research — not shipping

  • Multi-channel EMG
  • Flexible electrode pads for comfort
  • Muscle-pattern recognition toward multi-gesture and per-finger control
