Third-year embedded systems engineering student at Burapha University, Thailand. Co-founder and CTO of MIONEX, where six myoelectric arms are on six people. The rest of my time goes to robots that have to work on a competition floor.

Principal work

MIONEX — a myoelectric arm for below-elbow amputees.

I designed the control board, the firmware, and the single-channel surface EMG front end, and I fitted the arms to their users. Six arms were delivered under a government contract that is now complete. Thai PBS put the arm on national television.

A single EMG channel, done carefully, is enough to give someone a grip they can rely on — that is the result our users scored highest. They also score the weight lowest, and we do not argue with them. It is what sets the research agenda.
